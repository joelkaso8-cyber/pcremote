Edit apps & shortcuts

Add, change, reorder and delete the buttons on your phone with clicks instead of code. No commas or backslashes to get wrong.

Step 1

Bring in your current file

  1. On the PC, open PC Remote (next to the clock) and click Edit apps & shortcuts. Notepad opens.
  2. In Notepad press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C. Leave Notepad open.
  3. Paste it below with Ctrl+V.

Your file stays on this computer. It holds your PIN, so this page never uploads it or saves it anywhere.
