How to set it up

On your computer?

Scan this with your phone's camera to open this page on your phone, for the Android app or the iPhone setup.

Everything from the couch

The things you get up for, now on your phone.

Made for gaming PCs

GPU and CPU load and temperatures, GPU hotspot, RAM, ping, your Steam and Epic games, clip recording, per-app volume, and force close for frozen games.

Volume, brightness, media

Slide the volume, dim the screen, and play, pause or skip songs and videos in any app.

Touchpad and keyboard

Move the mouse, click, scroll and drag with your finger. Type text and press keys like Esc, Tab and the arrows.

See your screen

A live picture of your PC screen on your phone. Tap the picture to click on that spot.

Apps and shortcuts

One tap opens Chrome, Spotify, a game or a website, or sends a shortcut like alt+tab. Includes a slideshow remote.

Sleep timer

Falling asleep to a film? The PC sleeps or shuts down by itself when the timer runs out.

Files and photos

Send photos, videos and files both ways in original quality. Browse your PC's folders with photo previews.

PC at a glance

CPU, memory, disk and battery, live on your phone, plus a shared clipboard.

Power, PIN protected

Lock, sleep, restart or shut down. Only phones with your PC's PIN can control it, and it all stays on your Wi-Fi.

Set up in 2 minutes

The PC and the phone must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Install on the PC

  1. Open PCRemoteSetup.exe.
  2. Click Yes when Windows asks for permission, then Install.
  3. PC Remote opens and shows the PC's PIN. Later you'll find it next to the clock.

Install on the phone

  1. Open PCRemote.apk on the phone.
  2. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file app.
  3. Tap Install.

Connect

  1. Open PC Remote on the phone.
  2. Tap your PC in the list.
  3. Type the PIN shown on the PC. Done.

Install on the PC

  1. Open PCRemoteSetup.exe.
  2. Click Yes when Windows asks for permission, then Install.
  3. PC Remote opens and shows the PC's address and PIN. Later you'll find it next to the clock.

Open it in Safari

  1. On the iPhone, open Safari.
  2. Type the address shown on the PC into the address bar.
  3. The remote opens. Nothing to install.

Add to Home Screen

  1. Tap Share . On newer iPhones, tap ⋯ first.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open PC Remote from your Home Screen and type the PIN shown on the PC. Done.

Seeing a warning?

That's normal for small free apps. Here's how to get past each one.

Browser

"Not commonly downloaded"

Chrome or Edge may hold the download. Open the downloads list, click ⋯ next to the file, and choose Keep, then Keep anyway.

Windows

"Windows protected your PC"

Click More info, then Run anyway.

Android

"Install unknown apps" or Play Protect

Tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source. If Play Protect warns, tap More details and then Install anyway.

These appear because PC Remote isn't sold through an app store and isn't signed with a paid certificate.

Help

The phone can't find or connect to my PC
  1. Check the phone and the PC are on the same Wi-Fi, and not a guest network.
  2. On the PC, open PC Remote and click Fix firewall.
  3. In the phone app, type the address shown on the PC instead of searching. On an iPhone, check the address in Safari matches the PC exactly.
  4. Some routers stop devices from talking to each other ("AP isolation" or "client isolation"). Turn it off in the router settings.
Is there an iPhone app?
Not in the App Store, and you don't need one. On an iPhone, PC Remote runs in Safari: open the address shown on the PC, then add it to your Home Screen to keep it as an icon. See the iPhone steps.
Where is PC Remote on my PC?
Look for the PC Remote icon in the tray next to the clock (you may need to click the ^ arrow). It's also on the desktop and in the Start menu.
How do I change the apps and shortcuts?
The easy way: open the apps & shortcuts editor on your PC. In PC Remote click Edit apps & shortcuts, copy the file that opens in Notepad into the editor, add or change things with buttons, then paste the result back, save, and on the phone tap ⋮ → Refresh. Your file never leaves your PC. Prefer to edit it by hand? An app looks like { "name": "Spotify", "icon": "🎧", "target": "spotify:" } and a shortcut like { "name": "Mute mic", "icon": "🎙️", "keys": "win+alt+k" }.
Brightness doesn't change my monitor
Laptop screens work right away. For a desktop monitor, turn on DDC/CI in the monitor's own menu. Without it, PC Remote dims the picture in software instead, and it goes back to normal when PC Remote closes.
CPU temperature or GPU hotspot shows "--"
Windows doesn't let normal apps read those sensors. Install a free sensor app on the PC and keep it running as administrator: HWiNFO (in its settings, turn on Shared Memory Support) or LibreHardwareMonitor. PC Remote picks up the temperatures from it by itself.
A shortcut doesn't work in one program
Shortcuts go to the window in front. Windows doesn't let them reach programs that run as administrator.
I don't want to install anything on my phone
Open the address shown on the PC in your phone's browser: Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone. You get the same remote in the browser. To keep it as an icon, use ⋮ → Add to Home screen in Chrome, or Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari.
Is it safe?
PC Remote only works on your own Wi-Fi and needs the PIN shown on your PC. After 5 wrong PINs, that phone is blocked for a minute. You can make a new PIN any time with New PIN in the PC app.
Something isn't working
  1. Make sure both apps are the newest version from this page: install them again over the old ones.
  2. On an old Android phone, update Android System WebView (or Chrome) from the Play Store. On an iPhone, update iOS to get the newest Safari.
  3. If the problem stays, PC Remote writes what went wrong to %APPDATA%\PCRemote\errors.log on the PC. Paste that address into File Explorer to find it.
How do I uninstall it?
On the PC: Settings → Apps, find PC Remote, and click Uninstall. On an Android phone: hold the PC Remote icon and choose Uninstall. On an iPhone: hold the icon and remove it from the Home Screen.
